BLOCKYROLL

Roll the slime ball along the floating
sky-bridge and through the nether portal at the end.
Every level is the same length — push the loose plank
down over the broken span and dodge what is left.
From level 4 a foam wall blocks the way: roll into it
until it bursts. Roll into the air and the level restarts.
